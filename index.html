📘 How to use these prompts

Pick a prompt, hit Copy, then paste into your favourite AI image tool. Most prompts work best with a reference photo attached.

  • Best tools: Midjourney, Flux, Stable Diffusion, Leonardo, Ideogram, GPT-4o, Grok, Gemini.
  • Reference photo: prompts marked "uploaded" or "preserving facial identity" need a clear, well-lit face photo for best results.
  • Aspect ratios: add --ar 4:5 for portraits / posters, --ar 3:4 for products, --ar 16:9 for posters.
  • Adjust freely: swap colours, change clothing, swap the location, or add small details — the prompt is a starting point, not a contract.
  • Tip: run the same prompt twice — AI tools vary, so you usually want the second generation, not the first.

❓ FAQ

Are these prompts free to use?

Yes. Every prompt on this page is free to use for personal and commercial projects. No sign-up, no attribution required.

Do I need an account?

No account here. You only need an account with the AI tool you paste the prompt into (Midjourney, Flux, etc.).

Can I use my own photo?

Yes — most prompts include an "uploaded photo" or "preserving facial identity" cue. Use only photos you own or have permission to use.

Is it okay to upload someone else's photo?

Only with their clear permission. Photos of friends, family, colleagues or public figures should not be uploaded to an AI tool without consent, and photos of children need a parent or guardian to agree.

Does this site see or store my photos?

No. This gallery only gives you text to copy. Any photo you attach goes straight to the AI tool you choose, so read that tool's privacy policy.

Can I use the resulting images commercially?

Generally yes for your own generations. Check the licence of the AI tool you use (Midjourney, Adobe Firefly, etc.) for the fine print — terms differ.

Which AI tool works best?

For photoreal portraits: Midjourney v6, Flux 1.1 Pro, or Stable Diffusion XL with a good realism checkpoint. For art styles: Midjourney, Leonardo Phoenix, or Ideogram.

Why are some prompts longer than others?

Longer prompts give more control over composition, lighting and style. Shorter prompts leave more to the AI's interpretation. Try both and see what fits your style.

💡 Quick tips

Want to change the look? Edit a few keywords — for example, swap "warm amber tones" for "cool teal tones" to instantly shift the mood.

Want a different subject? Replace "[image 1]" with your subject description, or change the gender, age, hair, or outfit mentioned in the prompt.

Common mistake: pasting a prompt without attaching the reference photo when the prompt asks for it. Most "preserving identity" prompts need that photo.

🤝 Using photos of real people responsibly

A prompt only describes a style. The photo you pair with it is what makes it personal, so a few habits are worth keeping.

  • Ask first. If someone else is in the photo, get a clear yes before you upload it, and take it down if they change their mind.
  • Children need a guardian's consent. Do not upload pictures of other people's children.
  • Keep it honest. Do not present an AI-edited image as a real photograph, and do not use these prompts to mock, harass, impersonate or mislead anyone.
  • Check the photo itself. Crop out documents, house numbers, screens and bystanders before you upload.
  • Know where the photo goes. It is sent to the AI tool you choose, under that tool's privacy rules, not to this site.
  • No photo needed for a look. Swap the reference for a written description of an imaginary person and the style still works.
  • Names and logos. Prompts here describe styles in general terms. If you add a brand, character or logo yourself, you are responsible for respecting its trademark.
